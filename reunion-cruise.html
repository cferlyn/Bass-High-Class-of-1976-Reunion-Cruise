GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the group rate?

The listed price covers the following:

  • Stateroom
  • Access to all non-fee dining rooms and buffets , entertainment, and ship amenities
  • Unlimited Open Bar package, which includes unlimited soft drinks, wine, beer, and spirits up to $15 
  • Special Dining — 1 Meal Credit ($40 total per stateroom)
  • Excursion Credits — $50 credit toward every shore excursion 
  • Wi-Fi package — 150 minutes of high-speed Wi-Fi per person

Do I need to put down a deposit?

Yes — depending on the type of stateroom, a deposit of $125.00  $150.00 per person secures your cabin at the group rate. It's fully refundable until June 18, 2027, per Norwegian Cruise Line’s cancellation policy.

Can I bring my spouse, kids, or a friend who didn't go to Bass High School?

Absolutely — this trip is open to classmates and their guests. Just let the group coordinator know how many are in your party when you book.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellation terms follow Norwegian Cruise Line’s standard policy for the fare type you book. Ask the group coordinator for the exact deadlines tied to our sailing.

Are flights or hotel stays included?

No — this group rate covers the cruise only. You're responsible for arranging your own travel to and from Port Canaveral, Florida.
